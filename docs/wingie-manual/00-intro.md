# Sleepcousonique on the Wingie

### *a dream journal for a handheld resonator*

**Wingie mode · firmware 1.5.0-beta1 · editor 1.5**

> **sleep·cou·so·nique** /sliːp.ku.sɔ.nik/ *n.* — a patch language written like a
> dream journal. Each patch is a **dream**, its modules are **places**, and the cords
> between them are **passages**. Until now it slept only in the SP-1. Now it also
> sleeps in a **Meng Qi Wingie2**: two microphones, two little keyboards, three faders,
> and a resonator that hears the room.

The Wingie2 is a listening instrument. You hold it, it hears what is around you, and it
rings. Sleepcousonique keeps the listening and opens the inside: the same engine that runs
on the SP-1, with **237 places**, now runs on the Wingie's ESP32, and a dream can be
anything from the Wingie's own resonator to a granular field, a spectral freeze, a dub
echo or Alvin Lucier's room.

- **12 dream slots** on the Wingie, chosen from the panel
- **scenes** inside every dream, stepped with the Mode buttons
- the Wingie's own resonator and caves as places (`wingie`, `cave`), plus Ciat-Lonbarde
  (`coco`, `cocoquantus`, `petals`, `rollz`), hi-fi granular, spectral, time and colour places
- **the hands are yours**: the keys, the Mode buttons, the octave and source switches and all
  three faders mean whatever the dream says
- the editor draws the Wingie's panel, plays it from the computer keyboard, listens through the
  computer's microphone, and shows what the real Wingie measures, every second
- a dream that is too heavy doesn't break up: the Wingie goes back to **the last dream that fit**

It is still your Wingie: the stock firmware goes back on with Meng Qi's web flasher whenever
you like.

## 0. The dream in thirty seconds

1. Flash the firmware (chapter 1). The Wingie wakes up playing **first light**: the
   microphones ring two sets of sympathetic strings.
2. Play the keys: the left keyboard tunes the left strings, the right keyboard the right.
   **Decay** (the middle fader) is how long they ring.
3. In the editor, **File ▸ New dream for the Wingie**, write a few lines, and press
   **send to Wingie**. It plays at once and stays in the slot.
4. Hold **both Mode buttons** and press a key on the left keyboard: C is slot 1, B is
   slot 12.
5. Hold the **left Mode** button and tap the **right** one: the next scene of the dream.

The rest of this manual is the long night.
